Calculation used

For a solid circular specimen, J = πd⁴/32. Fit the selected initial readings to T = a + mθ, using torque in N·mm and θ in radians, then calculate G = mL/J.

Conversions: 1 kgf·cm = 98.0665 N·mm; 1 kgf·m = 9806.65 N·mm.

The default of 7 points is only a starting point. Compare a few more and fewer points and inspect the fitted graph. A high R² does not by itself prove that the selected points are physically elastic.
